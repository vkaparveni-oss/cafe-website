About us

A neighborhood cafe built for connection.

Velvet Bean began with a simple idea: create a cozy corner where great coffee and good company feel effortless. We brew small-batch beans, bake fresh pastries daily, and serve meals that feel comforting and elevated.

Every detail is designed to slow you down — from the warm wood textures to the rich aroma of our espresso bar.
